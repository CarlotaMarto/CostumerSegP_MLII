Data Science Note: Feature Merging

During our initial exploratory analysis, we discovered very high correlations between specific product categories, notably Meat & Fish and Videogames & Electronics.

However, after several iterative tests, we verified that these variables independently lacked strong predictive influence on customer behavior. To optimize the model's performance and reduce dimensionality, we merged these highly correlated categories into unified features.

Feature Engineering Highlights

We transformed raw data into meaningful insights. Here are the key engineered features that power our models:

Education Level

Extracted directly from titles (Bsc, Msc, Phd). Set to 'Unknown' when missing.

Family Structure

⏱️

Converted absolute lifetime spend into percentages (e.g., % on Groceries) to capture pure customer preferences independent of their overall budget scale.

Advanced Modeling & Projections

Model validation metrics and dimensionality reduction projections from our Python Notebooks.

Elbow Method

Insight: The "elbow" indicates the optimal number of clusters where the rate of variance drop slows down. We identified 8 clusters as the sweet spot.

Silhouette Score

Insight: Measures how similar an object is to its own cluster compared to others. A higher score at k=8 confirms well-separated, distinct customer personas.

Hierarchical Dendrogram

Insight: Visualizes the arrangement of clusters produced by hierarchical clustering, validating the major splits in customer behavior.

UMAP Projection

Insight: UMAP excellently captures the non-linear, complex topology of our customer base, clearly segregating the 8 distinct clusters while preserving both local and global structure.

t-SNE Projection

Insight: t-SNE expands dense regions to show fine-grained local variations. The solid color groupings reaffirm that our K-Means clusters represent genuinely distinct behavioral patterns.
